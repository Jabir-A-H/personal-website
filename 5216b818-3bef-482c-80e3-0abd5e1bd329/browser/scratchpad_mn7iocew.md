## Observations
- **Home Page**:
    - Clean, bold hero section.
    - Strong hierarchy with large typography.
    - Consistent solaris tint and accent color (`#e8915a`).
    - Contact links and bio are well-spaced.
- **Experience Page**:
    - Consistent with the "solaris" theme.
    - Large, clear headers.
    - Information is well-organized with dividers.
    - Maintains the professional, clean aesthetic.
- **Projects Page**:
    - Dramatic shift to "Dark Mode" / "Cyber" theme.
    - Uses grid background, monospaced fonts, and uppercase labels.
    - Feel: Technical, high-precision, "Terminal" aesthetic.
    - Visual hierarchy is strong with oversized titles and system-like indicators (LAT/LNG, SYSTEM ONLINE).
- **Whispers Page**:
    - Returns to the light "solaris" theme.
    - Minimalist, elegant layout with a vertical timeline.
    - Uses a combination of serif headers and sans-serif content for a "journal" feel.
    - Hashtags are styled as subtle pills.
- **Contact Page**:
    - Split-screen layout: context on the left, action items on the right.
    - High-impact typography for "ENDPOINTS".
    - Hover effects on contact items add interactivity.
    - Clean, modern, and direct.

## Visual Design Audit Summary

Overall, the website is a masterclass in balance between professional clarity and creative expression.

**Strengths:**
- **Typography**: Excellent use of font pairings (Serif for elegance/emotion, Sans-serif for clarity, Mono for technicality).
- **Color Palette**: The "solaris" theme (warm neutral + orange accent) is unique and cohesive.
- **Consistency**: Most pages share the same underlying grid and spacing logic.
- **Personality**: The dramatic shift on the Projects page is a bold design choice that works perfectly to highlight technical "hacker" skills without breaking the overall brand.

**Recommendations:**
- **Load Times**: Some pages (Education, Projects) feel slightly slow to load initial content. Consider optimizing animations or data fetching.
- **Projects Theme**: While the dark theme is great, ensure the "Home" and "Back" navigation experience doesn't feel too jarring for non-technical users.
